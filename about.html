About us

A live classroom that pays people for what they know.

Growmie is an EdTech platform from Growmie Software Limited. We help creators, instructors, and experts monetize their knowledge by hosting live classes — and we help learners find those rooms.

Whether you’re teaching a skill, sharing insights, or building a learning community, Growmie gives you the tools to earn, engage, and grow.

Flexible payments, affiliate rewards, class scheduling, and global reach sit in one app. The idea is simple: make it easier for anyone, anywhere, to teach and earn — and just as easy to show up as a student.
