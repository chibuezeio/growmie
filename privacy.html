Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 2025. Owned and operated by Growmie Software Limited.

Welcome to Growmie, a global platform enabling creators and instructors to host live classes and monetize their knowledge. We take your privacy seriously and handle personal data with care.

1. Information we collect

We collect personal and non-personal data to provide, improve, and secure our services.

1.1 Personal information

  • Full name, email address, and phone number
  • Billing information
  • Nationality and country of residence
  • Government-issued ID where required
  • Social media handles if linked

1.2 Usage and device information

  • IP address, browser type, and operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent, clickstream data, and session logs

1.3 Payment and instructor data

  • Payment method details via secure third-party processors
  • Purchase and transaction history
  • Course details, revenue reports, affiliate links, and referral metrics

2. How we use your information

  • To provide access to the platform and services
  • To personalize your experience
  • To process transactions and payouts
  • To send updates, notifications, and reminders
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and illegal activity
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To improve support and the product
  • For analytics and marketing with your consent

3. Legal bases for processing (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, we process data under contractual necessity, legitimate interests, consent, or legal obligation, depending on the context.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with trusted service providers, legal authorities when required, affiliated companies within Hamrex Group under confidentiality, and other users only as needed to run a class or referral.

5. Storage and international transfers

Growmie operates globally. Transfers use Standard Contractual Clauses and other lawful safeguards.

6. Security

We use SSL encryption, audits, layered server protection, role-based access, and secure payment gateways. No system is 100% secure — use a unique password and watch your account.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may access, correct, delete, restrict, port, or object to processing of your data, and withdraw consent. Contact privacy@growmieapp.com.

8. Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above, legal obligations, and financial reporting (usually up to 7 years), then delete or anonymize it.

9. Cookies

Cookies help remember login state, track usage, personalize the product, and — with consent — deliver relevant ads. You can control cookies in your browser.

10. Third-party links

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of linked third-party sites or services.

11. Children’s privacy

Growmie is not intended for users under 13 (or 16 in some jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

12. Changes

We may update this policy, change the effective date, and notify you of significant updates. Continued use means you accept the revised policy.
